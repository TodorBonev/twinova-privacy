Privacy Policy

Twinova — a puzzle game for Android by Virevon · com.virevon.twinova
Effective 3 November 2026

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. I do not run a server for the game.
  • Your progress, stars, boosters, settings and daily streak are stored only on your phone. I never receive these saved files.
  • I do not sell your data for money and do not collect your name, email or account data. Google’s ads may use data for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as selling or sharing; you can opt out in Settings → Privacy options.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect data for that, as described below. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • The game downloads a few numbers (how often ads may appear) from Firebase Remote Config.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • The game is not directed to children.

1. Who I am

Twinova is made by Virevon (me). If you have any question about this policy, write to me at quantumesqy@gmail.com.

2. What is stored on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps a few small files in its private storage on your phone:

  • your progress: which levels you completed and the stars you earned, and your daily challenge record and streak;
  • the level you have started but not finished;
  • your boosters, whether you bought Remove ads, and which purchases were already delivered;
  • your settings: language, sound, vibration, colour-blind patterns and reduced motion.

These files are never sent to me or to anyone else. Other apps cannot read them. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android’s settings. If you have backup turned on, Android may copy them to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup); that is between you and Google.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Twinova is free. It is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, using Google’s Mobile Ads SDK. Optional in-app purchases, Remove ads and booster packs, are available (see section 5).

  • Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one to get +5 moves, a free booster for one attempt, or a Resonance scan. You get the reward only if you watch the ad to the end. They stay available if you bought Remove ads.
  • Interstitial ads may appear between levels, only after you have reached level 15, and never if you bought Remove ads.

Ads are limited to content rated up to PG (parental guidance) in AdMob.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use information such as your Android advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location that can be derived from it), device and operating-system details, the app’s name and version, and how you interact with ads and whether they load. Google uses this to choose and show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and abuse, and — depending on your consent and the rules where you live — to personalise ads. Google is responsible for this data. I have no access to it; from AdMob I receive only totals such as the number of ads shown and the revenue.

Your consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area (which includes Bulgaria) and the United Kingdom, the game shows Google’s consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. Depending on your choice, ads may be personalised, non-personalised or not shown. In US states with privacy laws a similar choice is offered.
  • Where this form is shown, you can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • On your phone you can reset or delete the advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in Android’s settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path depends on your phone).

More about how Google handles this information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Settings from the internet (Firebase Remote Config)

The game asks Firebase Remote Config, a service from Google, for a few numbers that decide how often interstitial ads may appear. This lets me change them without a new version of the game. The numbers are fetched in the background and used the next time the game starts.

To answer, Firebase receives the installation ID (a random identifier of this installation of the game), your IP address, and the app version and device details that every internet request carries. No account, name, email address or advertising ID is involved, and the game works without the answer.

This data is processed by Google. More about how Google handles it: Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can buy Remove ads and booster packs inside the game. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. I never see your card details, name or address; Google only tells the game that the purchase went through, which the game remembers on your phone as described above. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Google Play. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

6. What the game does not do

  • It has no account, log-in or user profile.
  • It has no tracking or cookies of its own. (The ads described in section 3 and the settings described in section 4 are Google’s and may use their own identifiers, cookies or similar technologies.)
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It has no chat or social features and does not show other players’ data.

The game asks Android for these permissions:

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to load ads and settings and to check the connection. Used by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK and Firebase.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): the advertising ID, used for ads as described in section 3.
  • Android’s ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): Android’s Privacy Sandbox ad services, used by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK where your device supports them.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): the optional purchases through Google Play.
  • Keeping the device awake and running a short task (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by the background-task library (WorkManager) of Google’s Mobile Ads SDK, to finish short background tasks. The game itself does not run in the background.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): haptic feedback only, which you can turn off in Settings.

The game does not ask for any other permission, such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

7. Sharing

I do not sell your data for money. The only other parties involved are Google (ads, Remote Config and payments), as described above, which act under their own privacy policies. Google’s ads may use data for personalised ads, which some US state laws treat as selling or sharing; you can opt out in Settings → Privacy options.

8. Children

Twinova is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law where you live, for example 16 in some EU countries). I do not knowingly collect any information from children, and the game itself asks for no name, email or account details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the game in a way that concerns you, contact me and I will help.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data that Google collects for ads and Remote Config is kept and can be managed as described in Google’s policies and in your Google account and Android settings.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom or some US states) you may have rights over personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, objection and withdrawing consent. Because I keep no account and no details that identify you, there is little for me to access or delete myself; for data Google collects through ads and Remote Config, use the choices above, your Google account controls, or contact Google. You can withdraw ad consent at any time as described in section 3. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. You are welcome to contact me with any question.

11. Security and international processing

Your game data sits in the game’s private storage on your phone and is protected by Android. Payments, ads and Remote Config are handled by Google, which processes data in many countries under its own safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

When I change this policy I will update the date at the top of this page. The current version is always available at this address.

За тези данни отговаря Google. Аз нямам достъп до тях; от AdMob получавам само общи суми, като брой показани реклами и приходи.

4. Настройки от интернет (Firebase Remote Config)

Играта пита Firebase Remote Config, услуга на Google, за няколко числа, които определят колко често могат да се появяват междинните реклами. Така мога да ги променям без нова версия на играта. Числата се изтеглят на заден план и се ползват при следващото стартиране на играта.

За да отговори, Firebase получава идентификатора на инсталацията (случаен идентификатор на тази инсталация на играта), IP адреса ти, версията на приложението и данните за устройството, които носи всяка интернет заявка. Не участват акаунт, име, имейл адрес или рекламен идентификатор, а играта работи и без отговора.

Тези данни се обработват от Google. Повече за това как Google работи с тях: Политика за поверителност на Google.
